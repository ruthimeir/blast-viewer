Result column reference
qseqid
Name or ID of your read (Query), e.g. SRR33263609.1060.
sseqid
ID of the matching sequence in the database (Subject / GenBank ID).
pident
Percentage of identity: how many of the nucleotides in the aligned region are identical between your read and the database sequence.
length
Length of the aligned region in bases (bp).
mismatch
Number of mismatches in the aligned region.
gapopen
Number of gap openings in the aligned region.
qstart / qend
Start and end positions of the alignment within your read.
sstart / send
Start and end positions of the alignment within the database subject sequence.
evalue
Statistical significance (Expect value). A very small value (e.g. 2.5e-12 or 0.0) means the hit is real and not due to chance.
bitscore
Normalized alignment score. A higher score means a better, stronger match.
stitle
Subject title: the full taxonomic lineage (domain, phylum, class…) down to the species name. Here it is split into domain, species and lineage columns.
